One cafe · Two appetites

Fresh, high-quality, and for everybody.

Bearcat is a full-service sit-down cafe with a heavy emphasis on fresh breakfast and lunch. We carry a unique array of sustainable, micro-sourced coffees, a wide variety of loose-leaf teas, and house-made bottled beverages.

The menu splits two ways: Good Cat for lighter fare, Bad Cat for the hearty, indulgent dishes. Vegetarian, vegan, paleo, and gluten-free choices run through all of it.

We try to cater to each and every patron to the best of our abilities.

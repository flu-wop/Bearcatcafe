One cafe.
Two appetites.

Bearcat is a full-service sit-down cafe built on fresh, high-quality breakfast and lunch — a curated line of sustainably-sourced coffee, loose-leaf teas, and house-made bottled beverages alongside a menu that genuinely means it when it says "for everyone."

Every menu splits in two: Good Cat for lighter, vegetable-forward plates, and Bad Cat for the hearty, indulgent side of the house. Vegetarian, vegan, paleo, and gluten-free options run through both.

Three concepts carry the mark across the city: Bearcat Uptown, Bearcat CBD — with a full dinner menu and bar program — and Bearcat Baked, a dedicated bakery and coffee counter.
